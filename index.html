The brand

Security should help people decide.

DemoniX is developing practical security products for individuals and teams. We explain why a message looks suspicious, protect user choice, and test our claims before asking people to rely on them.

The problem

Pause before a click or payment.

Phishing pages look polished. Fake offers arrive through trusted platforms. Fraudulent links are engineered to trigger panic, urgency, and one bad click.

A useful warning should explain its evidence and guide independent verification. A low-risk result must never be treated as proof that a message is safe.

Product 01 / AI Shield

A second look at suspicious messages.

AI Shield is an advisory scam checker for UPI collect requests, banking messages, emails, chats and webpage text. It shows a 0–100 risk score, reasons and a safer next step.

Open web demo Get offline extension View source ↗

The Chrome extension processes scans locally after your click and has no scan history. The web demo has separate optional API, logging and OCR workflows. Chrome Web Store publication is pending.

01

For individuals

Check a UPI PIN or collect-request message before acting. A SAFE result still calls for verification through an official channel.

02

For banks and fintech teams

Evaluate the shared offline detection engine with consented test data in a scoped pilot before making deployment claims.

03

Honest limits

The advisory detector can miss scams or raise false alarms. Image-only tricks, redirects and authenticated sender headers need further work.
